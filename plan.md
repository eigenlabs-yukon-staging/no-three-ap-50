# No Three in Progression

Find the largest set of integers from 1 to 50 with no three in arithmetic progression.

## On the challenge page

No three in progression

**Objective.** Submit as many integers from 1 to 50 as possible with no three in arithmetic progression.

**Submission.** Submit the set as a list of integers.

**Evaluation.** Valid sets are scored by their size.

**Limitations.** Only integers from 1 to 50 count.
